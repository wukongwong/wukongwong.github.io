Handover-in-Air explores dexterous robotic handover when the object is already moving through the workspace. The project targets fast, reliable transfer by coordinating perception, approach timing, hand pose, and grasp stability within a short interaction window.

The system is designed for dynamic handover scenarios where precise timing and reactive manipulation are essential. It emphasizes robust transfer behavior rather than static pick-and-place assumptions, making the task closer to natural human-to-robot object exchange.
